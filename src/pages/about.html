Credentials & approach

Sally is a Montana native who helps clients build “Montana Strong” lives—steady habits that hold up through busy seasons and changing bodies. She is nationally certified in personal training through the American College of Sports Medicine, with TRX suspension training and STOTT PILATES® instruction; she weaves the five foundational STOTT principles into sessions when Pilates is part of your plan.

Family, faith & balance

Sally and her husband Alan raised three sons here; their family now includes two daughters-in-law and a granddaughter she adores. Faith and family anchor her life—and she brings that same grounded outlook to coaching: nutrition counseling, movement, music, and nature as partners to everyday wellness. She believes staying healthy across every phase of life supports physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Beyond the studio

Away from sessions, Sally makes the most of Montana’s outdoors and hosts Fit 4 Montana on Big Valley Radio—conversations on wellness under the Big Sky. You might find her hiking, skiing, snowshoeing, biking, on horseback, fly fishing, singing, strength training, practicing Pilates, cooking, or in the garden with family and friends.
