Meet your coach

Montana born. Montana strong.

Sally Beaulieu is a Montana native who helps people build “Montana Strong” lives—steady habits that hold up through busy seasons and changing bodies.

She’s nationally certified through the American College of Sports Medicine, with STOTT PILATES® and TRX training. Her approach is simple: listen first, adjust often, and keep you in the driver’s seat.

Faith and family anchor her life. She and her husband Alan raised three sons here; the family now includes two daughters-in-law and a granddaughter she adores. Sally believes staying healthy through every phase of life supports physical, mental, and spiritual well-being—and that music, nature, and good food belong in the plan.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Don’t see yours? Ask Sally directly—she reads every message.

Do I need to be in shape before I start? +

Not at all. Training meets you where you are today—whether that’s your first workout in years or getting ready for a big hike—and progresses as you do.

I have an old injury or back pain. Can I still train? +

Often, yes—and it’s worth bringing up at your consultation. Sally will ask about your history, pay attention to how your body responds, and change direction when something doesn’t feel right. If you’re working with a doctor or physical therapist, bring their guidance along.

What happens at the free consultation? +

A relaxed conversation about your goals, your health and activity history, and how movement fits into your week. You’ll come away with a clear picture of what training together would look like. It’s free.

I don’t live in Whitefish. Can I still work with Sally? +

Yes. Virtual coaching brings the same personal approach to wherever you are—across the Flathead Valley or across the country.

What is STOTT PILATES®? +

A contemporary approach to Pilates built on five basic principles: breathing, pelvic placement, rib cage placement, scapular movement, and head and neck placement. It builds core strength, posture, and control, and can be adapted to many fitness levels—so it fits into a lot of plans.
